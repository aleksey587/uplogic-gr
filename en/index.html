Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you should know before we start.

1. How fast will my website or online store be delivered?
Starter Landing Pages (from €350) are delivered starting from 3 business days. Comprehensive corporate websites take 5–8 days, modern e-commerce stores take 7–12 days, and AI Chatbots take 3–5 days. Every quote includes a binding written timeline guarantee.
2. Do I own 100% of the code? Are there hidden monthly fees?
Yes, the website, e-shop, or custom software belongs 100% to you. We deliver full repository access to your code, assets, and database credentials with zero vendor lock-in and zero mandatory monthly maintenance contracts.
3. How do you handle web hosting, domains, and SSL?
We configure your domain connection (`.gr`, `.com`, `.eu`, `.io`), activate automated SSL certificates (HTTPS), and deploy on high-speed global Edge CDN Cloud Hosting. All credentials belong to your company so you retain full control.
4. Why do you insist on 100% Custom Code & Zero Bloatware?
Generic themes and site builders load dozens of unnecessary scripts and plugins that slow down mobile load times and create security vulnerabilities. By engineering clean custom architecture, we achieve 99+ Google PageSpeed, sub-second global loading, and significantly higher Google search rankings.
5. How do we get started and how do I schedule a discovery call?
You can use the Interactive 3-Step Calculator above or click «📅 Book a 15' Discovery Call» to choose your preferred day, time, and channel (Phone, Google Meet, or WhatsApp) to speak directly with our engineering team.
